# The owner's week

George and Gus. You see everything and can change everything. You are also the only two who see who was responsible for a rework.

## Every day, two minutes

1. Open **Attention**. The number on the tab is how many things need a human: flags, overdue steps, dates that break a rule, weeks that are overbooked, jobs short of materials. Work the list top to bottom; each item has its answer next to it (**OK as is**, **Change dates**, **New date**, **Fixed**).
2. Glance at the **Board** for the week. Faded cards are provisional dates nobody has confirmed yet. Red cards are rework.

## Monday

- The Monday email lists everyone's week and the installs finished last week with their photos. Read it before the production meeting.
- **Pipeline** is the meeting screen: every live job in one row, soonest install first, the dark outline on the stage each job is at.
- **Load** shows the next six weeks of factory and install hours booked against hours available, day by day. A red day is overbooked.

## When a site manager signs off an install

Gus's My day shows **Needs your sign-off** when an installer has marked a job finished from their side. Inspect, then tap **Done**: gaps adjusted? site left clean? and your photos. That tick is the official completion; the installer's button is not.

## Monthly

- **Rework** has the monthly tally and, for owners only, **Who was responsible**. Record it on each rework as it closes; the tally is only as good as that.
- Check **Settings → People**: who has signed in, who has no areas set (the menu says so), installers who should be switched off.

## Only owners can

- Make someone an owner, or change an owner's record.
- See and record who was responsible for a rework.
- Make and revoke the Buildertrend bot's API tokens (Settings → Buildertrend bot).
