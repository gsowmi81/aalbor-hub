# Factory TV

A screen on the wall opens the factory's week with a link instead of a sign-in: rows for Materials in, Factory (cut, painting, delivery) and Install, this week by day and next week as a list, the office message, and the overdue count. It refreshes every two minutes. Read only.

Settings → Factory TV makes the link; making a new one stops the old. Keep it to the factory. A signed-in person can open More → Factory TV without the link.
