# The office's week (Christine)

You are the hub's editor. New jobs, every date, materials and the order list, the bot's suggestions and the settings are yours. Most of your week is the Board, Attention and the Suggestions screen.

## Every morning

1. **Attention.** Overdue steps and dates that break a rule, grouped by job, soonest install first. For an overdue step: **New date** opens the Reschedule screen with the rest of the job following. For a rule it can't keep: **OK as is** if the dates are right anyway, **Change dates** otherwise.
2. **Suggestions** (More → Suggestions; the count is on the More tab). The Buildertrend bot posts what it found overnight: a date that doesn't match, a new job, a status change. **Accept** applies it and ticks the date Confirmed; **Decline** leaves the hub as it is; **Buildertrend is wrong** records that Buildertrend needs fixing, not the hub.
3. The **Board** for anything that moved. Drag a card sideways to move it to another day; the box asks what happens to the rest of the job.

## When a job is sold

More → **New job**: job type, job number, client and suburb, and the one date you know (site start, or the install if that is what the client has been promised). **Show proposed dates** fills every other date from the job type's rules and shows which factory and install weeks the job lands in and how full they are. Save. Every date is provisional until you confirm it. See [A job from start to finish](/help/job-start-to-finish).

## When plans arrive

On the job, **Files → Add a file**: contract plans (what the client signed) or production plans (signed off, build from these). A new file of the same class becomes the current version; the old one stays as superseded. The job sheet and the installer's site page show the current ones.

## Materials for a job

Once the production plans are in, on the job's **Materials** section load the Cabinet Vision cut list (or type the lines), then **Source**: the hub takes free stock first, then stock already on order, and lists the rest on the order list by the date it must be ordered. See [Materials for a job](/help/materials).

## Monday: the order list

More → Stock → **Order list** is grouped by supplier. **Ordered** with the expected date when you place it; **In** when it arrives (a partial stays on order). **Need and supply** is the six-week view behind it. See [The weekly stock cycle](/help/weekly-stock).

## Ticks you own

Materials ordered, on each job, once the order is placed. Confirmed, on each date, once it matches Buildertrend (moving a date confirms it for you).

## Settings you keep

Blocked days (public holidays are loaded to 2028; add shutdowns and clean-outs), the office message on My day and the Board, flag types and who looks after them, installer links on each job.
