# The site manager's week

You check measure, keep an eye on installs, raise rework for site problems and sign off each install. Gus runs the site teams.

## Every day

The hub opens on **My day**: your check measures and installs overdue, today, this week and next, one **Done** button each. **Flags for you** are the problems you look after, three at a time, the rest on Attention.

## Check measures

Tick **Done** on the check measure when it's done. Add a note if the measure changed anything. If it changes the dates, flag it (drawing query) so the office moves them.

## When an installer says they're finished

**Needs your sign-off** at the top of My day lists installs the installer has marked finished from their side, with a link to their log and photos. Inspect. Then tap **Done**: gaps adjusted? site left clean? and your photos. That is the official completion; nothing else is.

## Something wrong on site

Open the job (This job, or the job sheet's QR) and **Flag a problem** with photos, or raise rework: More → **Rework** → Raise rework, pick the job and the cause, tick what needs doing. The office dates it; it appears in red on the Board.

## The installer's link

Each job has a **Site** section with the installer's daily log, photos and anything they reported missing. The office makes and stops the links; ask Christine for one when a crew starts.
