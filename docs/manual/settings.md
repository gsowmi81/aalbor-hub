# Settings

More → Settings (Editors). A menu of pages, each with a line saying where things stand; a warning in orange means something is set wrong (people with no areas, a flag type with nobody responsible, no Factory TV link).

## People

One card per person: name, email, role, **Can sign in**, **Gets emails**, **Runs stock**, **Owner** (owners only), and their **areas** as chips. Areas are the Board rows a person works in; they make up My day. Switch someone off rather than deleting them, so history keeps their name. **Add a person** at the bottom. **Installers** who sign in by a job link are listed separately with the jobs their links are on.

Roles: **Editor** changes anything. **Doer** ticks steps, raises rework, flags, and on the floor counts, receives and picks. **Viewer** looks. The database enforces these, not just the screen.

## Capacity

Hours a day per weekday for the Factory and the Install crew, and periods with different hours (a shutdown is 0, someone on leave is less, an extra pair of hands is more).

## Job types

What a new job gets by default. Changes apply to new jobs only.

## Durations and limits

The working-day rules behind proposed dates: days for drawings, materials, the factory, painting, and the hours in a factory or install day.

## Blocked days

Public holidays (loaded to 2028), shutdowns and clean-outs. "Everyone" days stop every step; "Factory only" days stop factory steps.

## Flagged problems

Each kind of problem and who looks after it. A flag goes to that person and the Editors.

## Emails

The 7 am run: what it would send today, the recent runs, and **Send these now**.

## Message

One line at the top of My day and the Board.

## Factory TV

The link for the screen on the wall. **Make a new link** stops the old one.

## Buildertrend bot

Where the bot's batches are emailed. Owners also make and revoke the bot's API tokens here; a token is shown once.
