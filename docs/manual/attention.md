# Attention

Everything that needs a human, grouped by job, soonest install first. The number on the tab is the count. Doers see it under More.

## In order

1. **Flagged problems**: open flags, who looks after each, **Looking at it** / **Fixed** (with a note) / **Raise rework for this**.
2. **Short for the factory**: jobs whose materials are short, or on order for after the factory date.
3. **Per job**: overdue steps (**New date** opens the Reschedule screen; **Done** if it was done and nobody ticked it) and decisions.
4. **Capacity**: weeks and days with more hours booked than available.

## Needs a decision

A rule the dates don't keep: a step before the one it waits for, a pinned date clashed with, an install without a site start, an overbooked week. Each says what the hub would do about it.

- **OK as is** records that you looked and the dates are right. The item stays away until those dates change.
- **Change dates** opens the dates it's about.

Each group folds. Tap the job's name to open it.
