# Moving dates

Working days are Monday to Friday, and Saturday when the switch under Settings → Capacity is on; never Sundays, public holidays or blocked days. Dates depend on each other: check measure → drawings → materials ordered (soft) → materials in → factory → painting → delivery → install → stone measure → stone install. A move that breaks a hard rule needs a decision. Done steps never move. Pinned dates never move.

## Three ways to move one date

- **On the Board**, drag the card to another day (Editors, desktop). On a phone, **Move…** on the card.
- **On the job**, tap the date.
- **On Jobs**, drag the bar with a mouse, or tap it.

Each asks the same question: what happens to the rest of the job?

- **Move everything after it**: every later step moves by the same number of working days. The usual answer when the job has slipped.
- **Keep the install date**: the steps between this one and the install move only as far as the rules force, using spare days first. For when the client has been promised the install.
- **Only this one**: nothing else moves. For a date that was simply wrong.
- **See every date first** opens the Reschedule screen with every date editable before anything saves.

The move saves at once and confirms the dates it changed. If it would put a step before the one it waits for, nothing saves and the Reschedule screen opens with the clash; **Move anyway** if you mean it.

If someone else moved the step while your page was open, the move is refused and says so; reload and look again.

## The whole job

On the job, **Move the whole job**: −1 day, +1 day, +1 week for every open step. Rework and pinned dates stay.

## A promised date

**Pin** it on the job. Nothing moves it, and anything that clashes with it shows under Needs a decision.
