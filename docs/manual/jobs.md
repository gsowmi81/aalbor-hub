# Jobs (the chart)

Every live job from this week on, one line each, sorted by install, with every dated step as a bar. Orange means a date has passed and nobody ticked it. Week headings and job names stay put while you scroll.

- Tap a job name to open it.
- Tap a bar to pick a date for it on the Reschedule screen.
- **Editors, with a mouse: drag a bar** to move it. The same box as the Board asks what happens to the rest of the job, then saves, confirmed.
- **All · Aalbor · Origin** filter by brand.
