# My day

Your steps this week, one **Done** button each. Doers land here when they sign in. "Yours" means a step in one of your areas (set under Settings → People) or with your first name on it.

## Sections, top to bottom

- **Needs your sign-off** (site managers): installs an installer has marked finished from their side, with their log and photos. **Done** here is the official sign-off.
- **Overdue**, **Today**, **Flags for you** (three, the rest on Attention), **Later this week**, **Next week**.

## Done

Tap **Done**. The step leaves the list and the office sees it. For ten seconds a line at the top says what you ticked with **Undo**. **Install cabinets** asks two questions first (gaps adjusted? site left clean?) and takes up to three photos.

## Flag

**Flag** under a step opens a short form: what's wrong, one line, photos. See [Flags](/help/flags).

## From the job sheet

Scanning the QR code on a job sheet opens that job's open steps, whoever's they are, each with Done and Flag. The plans are linked at the top.

## If it says your areas aren't set

You only see steps with your name on them. Ask George or Christine to tick your areas under Settings → People.
