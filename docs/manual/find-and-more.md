# Find a job, and More

**Find a job** in the header takes part of a job number, a client's name or a suburb. One match opens the job; more than one lists them.

**More** holds whatever your tab row doesn't: Rework, Load, Stock, Factory TV, Suggestions, New job, Settings, Spreadsheet, and this manual. The tab rows are by role: Editors Board · Pipeline · Attention · Jobs · More; the floor My day · This job · (Stock) · More; Viewers Board · Pipeline · Jobs · More.

**This job** is the job you last opened or scanned on that phone.

**Spreadsheet** downloads every job, step and rework as an Excel file.
