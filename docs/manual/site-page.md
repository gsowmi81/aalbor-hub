# The site page (installers)

What an installer sees from a job link: one job, no sign-in to the rest. The office makes the link on the job's Site section and sends it to the installer. See [Installers](/help/installer) for the installer's side.

For the office and site managers, everything they write appears on the job's **Site** section: daily log entries with photos, anything reported missing (also a flag, "Missing on site", to Gus), and "finished from the installer's side", which puts the job under **Needs your sign-off** on the site managers' My day.

**Stop** a link when the crew changes. Switching an installer off (Settings → People → Installers) stops every link they have. Either signs their phone out at once.
