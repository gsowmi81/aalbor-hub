# Monday production meeting: ten minutes on the hub

Pipeline on the big screen, the factory TV switched on, your phone in your hand. Say the first line, then show, don't describe.

**The line.** "Jobman's gone. The hub now does the factory, site and stock, and it tells each of you what's yours. Six things, then we're done."

1. **Pipeline** (on the screen). "Every live job, one row, soonest install first. The dark outline is where it's up to. This is what we'll look at in this meeting from now on."
2. **Tap a job.** Show the strip: Steps, Materials, Files, Flags, Site. Tap a date, show the move box, press Cancel. "Christine moves dates. Nobody else needs to."
3. **My day** (your phone, as a Doer: use Gus's). "This is what the factory and site see. Done. Undo. Flag." Tap Flag, show the form, close it.
4. **Attention** (back on the screen). "Everything that needs a human, in one list. Christine works it each morning. Flags you raise land here."
5. **The TV.** Point at it. "This week and next. It refreshes itself."
6. **Stock** (Need and supply, briefly). "Phil counts everything on Friday; until then these numbers are July's. Christine orders Mondays."

**What I need from you this week** (say each person's name):
- Phil: the Friday count, everything.
- Gus: the site managers' areas with me today; sign off the next install from My day.
- Christine: the Settings quarter hour this morning; one cut list loaded by Wednesday.
- Angela, Michael: open it once; tell me if anything's wrong.

**Close.** "The manual is under More → Manual, one page each. If something's wrong, flag it with 'Something else'; it lands on Attention and I'll see it the same day."
